# PhandaFin

The FREE Finance Tool For The Largely Underserved Informal/Small Business Setor In South Africa That Aims To Address Funding And Financial Exclusion Through Accessible, Easy-To-Use and Tailored Financial Record-Keeping.

### Every sale counts. Every business matters.

**Making financial recordkeeping accessible. Making financial futures possible.**

PhandaFin is a financial management web application designed to help informal traders, micro-enterprises and small businesses in South Africa digitise their transactions, simplify invoicing and build reliable financial histories.

We believe every business deserves access to simple, affordable financial tools — regardless of its size, resources or technical experience.

## The Problem

Many small businesses still rely on handwritten notebooks, memory or inconsistent recordkeeping to manage their finances.

This creates challenges:

- Lost or incomplete transaction records.
- Limited visibility into business performance.
- Difficulty generating professional invoices and financial reports.
- Barriers to accessing traditional accounting software.
- Insufficient financial documentation when applying for business funding.

A business can be commercially active yet financially undocumented.

## Our Solution

PhandaFin makes everyday financial recordkeeping simpler.

By turning routine business transactions into organised digital records, we aim to help entrepreneurs understand their finances, establish financial histories and become better prepared for funding opportunities.

**Record the transaction today. Build your financial history over time.**

## Who We Serve

PhandaFin is designed primarily for:

- Informal traders and street vendors.
- Spaza shops and small retailers.
- Township-based businesses.
- Freelancers and independent service providers.
- Home-based businesses.
- Emerging micro-enterprises.

Our initial focus is South Africa, with the potential to serve underserved small businesses across other African markets.

## Our Vision

We want to bridge the gap between informal economic activity and formal financial opportunity.

By making financial recordkeeping affordable, accessible and easier to maintain, PhandaFin aims to help small businesses build the financial visibility needed to make informed decisions and demonstrate their business performance.

Our long-term direction includes multilingual support, automated transaction capture, advanced financial reporting and funding-readiness tools.
